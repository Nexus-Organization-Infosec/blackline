# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Blackline to be a welcoming, professional, and inclusive project for
everyone who participates in it.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and give actionable feedback.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its community.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, doxxing, and deliberate
disruption are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's security reporting
process described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be handled
promptly and as confidentially as practical.
